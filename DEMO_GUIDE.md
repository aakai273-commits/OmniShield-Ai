# How to Test OmniShield AI MVP

1. Open the live prototype by clicking on `index.html`.
2. **Test Phishing Detection:** Type a suspicious message containing words like "تجميد حساب" (freeze account) or "رابط التحديث" (update link) and click the scan button to see the instant red alert.
3. **Test Vishing Call Protection:** Click the "بدء محاكاة مكالمة وهمية" (Start Mock Call) button to simulate a live voice scam interception with an automated safety disconnect.
