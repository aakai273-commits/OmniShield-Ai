# OmniShield AI - Pitch Deck Summary

## Slide 1: Title
* **Project Name:** OmniShield AI
* **Tagline:** Proactive Multi-Modal AI Shield Against Phishing & Deepfakes
* **Team:** Applied Technology High School (ATHS)

## Slide 2: The Problem
* Surge in sophisticated phishing links, voice scams (Vishing), and deepfakes targeting UAE residents.
* Traditional reactive tools only catch scams *after* financial or data loss happens.

## Slide 3: Our Solution
* Real-time, proactive multi-modal AI shield.
* Instant red-alert warnings before users interact with malicious links or fake voice calls.

## Slide 4: Architecture & AI Models
* **NLP & Text Analysis:** Detects malicious intent and phishing patterns in SMS/links.
* **Audio & Speech Analysis:** Tracks live call transcripts to flag bank impersonation and fraud keywords.
* **Local Dialect Support:** Fine-tuned to understand Gulf and Emirati dialects.

## Slide 5: Privacy by Design (Edge AI)
* All processing happens locally on the device (Edge AI).
* No personal calls or private messages are uploaded to external servers, complying with UAE data protection standards.

## Slide 6: Future Roadmap
* Integration with native Android/iOS CallKit APIs.
* Direct connection with official cybercrime reporting platforms for real-time threat updates.
